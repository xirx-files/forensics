    what are the various barricade sizes (in mm) that are used in utah and UVU for events

In Utah and at Utah Valley University (UVU), event barricades typically follow standard industry dimensions. While often listed in imperial units, their metric equivalents are used for precise planning. 
Common Steel Crowd Control Barricades
These "bike rack" style barriers are the most common type used for campus and state-wide events. 
Standard Size (8-foot): Approximately 2,438 mm (L) x 1,118 mm (H).
Compact Size (6.5-foot): Approximately 2,000 mm (L) x 1,100 mm (H).
Heavy Duty (8.5-foot): Approximately 2,591 mm (L) x 1,219 mm (H). 
Concert and Stage Barricades
For high-pressure areas like front-of-stage at major UVU concerts or festivals, "Mojo-style" aluminum barricades are standard. 
Typical Dimensions: 1,000 mm (W) x 1,200 mm (H).
Base Plate Depth: Often around 1,200 mm to provide a stable platform for security staff and attendees to stand on. 
Retractable Belt Barriers (Stanchions) 
Used primarily for indoor queue management, such as at UVU's Event Services check-in areas. 
Post Height: 1,000 mm.
Base Diameter: 320 mm to 350 mm.
Belt Length: Varies, but standard deployment is up to 3,000 mm. 
Temporary Fencing
For larger perimeter control at outdoor festivals or construction sites. 
Standard Panel: 2,400 mm (W) x 1,800 mm (H).
