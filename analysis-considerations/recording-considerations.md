    in the apple iphone 15 pro, is the left channel audio mic located on the top of the phone and the right audio channel mic located on the bottom of the phone when the phone is recording video in portrait mode


In the iPhone 15 Pro, the stereo audio channels are dynamically assigned based on the phone's orientation. 
When recording video in portrait mode (vertical):
Right Channel: The microphone located at the top of the phone (near the earpiece) typically acts as the right audio channel.
Left Channel: The microphone located at the bottom of the phone (near the charging port) typically acts as the left audio channel. 
Microphone Physical Locations
The iPhone 15 Pro features multiple microphones to achieve this stereo or spatial effect: 
Bottom: The primary microphone is at the bottom edge, right of the charging port.
Top/Front: A secondary microphone is integrated into the earpiece at the top.
Rear: A third microphone is located on the back near the camera lenses to capture audio from the direction of the subject. 
Key Behavior
Dynamic Orientation: The iPhone is designed to keep the "right" channel on top when held vertically. If you rotate the phone 180 degrees (upside down), the system reassigns the channels so the top-most microphone remains the right channel.
Landscape Mode: If you rotate to landscape, the channels shift so that the microphone on the side corresponding to the user's right side becomes the right channel.
Settings: You can enable or disable this feature by going to Settings > Camera > Record Stereo Sound. 


    when the phone is held vertically in standard (ie. not upside down position) portait mode, are both microphones perfectly centered on the device? or is the mic near the earpiece offset from center, or is the mic near the charging port offset from the center


In the iPhone 15 Pro, neither the top nor the bottom microphone used for stereo recording is perfectly centered when the phone is held in portrait mode. Both are offset from the device's vertical centerline. 
Bottom Microphone (Primary)
The primary microphone at the bottom is offset to the left of the charging port. 
Location: When looking at the bottom edge of the phone (screen facing you), the USB-C port is centered. To its right is a larger row of holes for the loudspeaker, and to its left is a smaller set of holes (typically one or two) for the primary microphone.
Impact: This offset means sound originating from the left side of the phone’s bottom edge is received more directly than sound from the right side. 
Top Microphone (Earpiece)
The top microphone used for stereo separation is offset from the center within the earpiece assembly. 
Location: This microphone is integrated into the narrow earpiece slit at the top of the display. While the slit itself is centered, the actual microphone sensor is positioned to one side of the internal speaker component to accommodate the Face ID sensors and front-facing camera. 
Additional Microphone
Rear: There is a third microphone located on the back of the device, specifically as a small dot near the camera lenses (between the Li-DAR sensor and the flash), which is also offset from the center of the phone. 
These technical diagrams and forum discussions confirm the offset positions of the iPhone 15 Pro's bottom and earpiece microphones in portrait mode.
