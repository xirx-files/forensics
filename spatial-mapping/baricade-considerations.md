Traffic Control Barricades (Type I, II, and III):
When events utilize public roadways (regulated by UDOT), specific dimensions are required for road closures. Type I and II must be at least 36 inches high, while Type III (used for full road closures) must be a minimum of 5 feet (60 inches) high and at least 4 feet wide.
